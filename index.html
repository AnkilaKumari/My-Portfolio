About Me

Hi, I’m Ankila Kumari, an MGIS student with a minor in Computer Science and a Graduate Assistant at U-Spatial

Before UMN, I worked at Cyient Pvt. Ltd. as an Associate GIS Specialist on satellite imagery and LRI projects.

I’m passionate about using GIS for transportation and healthcare research. I enjoy working with remote sensing, GEE, and building meaningful geospatial tools that support real-world decision-making.
